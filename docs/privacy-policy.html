Arrows ("the app") is a calm game published by Dante Berishaj ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles. It covers the Android app.

Data the app stores on your device

Your game progress (your current level, lifetime stats and streaks), your settings (sound and theme) and a few counters the game needs (for example, how many levels have passed since the last ad) are stored on your device. The app also stores a random number generated on your device and counts of app launches and app errors; in this version of the app they are not sent anywhere.

The app itself sends none of this data to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your device, Android can copy the app's stored data to the backup in your Google account, and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. We cannot see or access that backup. You control it in your Android settings and your Google account.

Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes the data on your device. A copy in your Google account's backup follows Google's backup rules until you delete it there.

Advertising

The app shows ads: an interstitial ad between some levels, and rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for a hint or to continue a level. The ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK; the app contains no other ad network or mediation service.

To show, measure and protect ads, Google's SDK collects the following from your device and sends it to Google, encrypted in transit:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • identifiers of your device: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID
  • how you interact with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the SDK perform

On Android versions that support them, the SDK can also use Android's own advertising features (the Privacy Sandbox, for example Topics and attribution reporting). Google uses this data for advertising, which can include personalised ads, for measurement and analytics, and to prevent fraud, under Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not receive this data from the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads; the wording differs between devices).

Lawful basis

This section applies where the GDPR or a similar law applies, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland.

  • Data stored on your device: we never receive it, so we need no lawful basis to process it; the app stores it only so the game works.
  • Advertising: where the law requires consent for the advertising processing described above, such as personalised ads, consent is the required basis. This version of the app does not show a consent message.

Retention

  • On your device: until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Android backup: under Google's backup rules and your backup settings.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under Google's policies (linked above); we hold no copy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, or a US state privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

  • We hold no personal data about you, and we cannot link the app on your device to you: there are no accounts, and the app sends us nothing. So we cannot look up, correct or delete data about you ourselves.
  • You can delete the data on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • For the advertising data Google holds, use the advertising ID settings above and the tools described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • To exercise a right, or with any question, email us at the address under Contact.

Do Not Sell or Share (US state privacy laws)

Some US state laws, for example California's, may treat personalised advertising through Google as "selling" or "sharing" personal information. You can opt out in two ways:

  • On your device: delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (see Advertising). Google's SDK reads these settings on your device.
  • By email: write to the address under Contact with "Do Not Sell or Share" in the subject. Because the app has no accounts and sends us nothing, we cannot identify your device, so we will reply with the on-device steps above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app does not ask for your age.
