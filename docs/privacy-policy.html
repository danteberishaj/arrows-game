Privacy Policy — Arrows

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Arrows ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Dante Berishaj. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app stores

Game progress (your current level, lifetime stats, and settings such as sound and theme) is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and collects no personal information itself.

Advertising

The app shows ads (interstitial ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads for hints and continues) served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), an ad mediation service. To serve and measure ads, the ad SDK may collect device information, including:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system, and app version
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by ironSource and its mediated ad networks under their own policies: ironSource / Unity LevelPlay privacy policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), which limits ad personalization.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new "last updated" date.
