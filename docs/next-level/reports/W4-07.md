# W4-07 — Record solved silhouettes into the collection (incremental, version-aware fold)

Implementer report, 2026-09-25. BASE = `8586adf`. Requirements: `task-W4-07-brief.md` (with its
binding controller amendments F11 and F13), `codex/GLOBAL.md`, and the controller additions in the

committed; the controller owns git.

> **Fix round 1 changed the menu fold.** The 200-level chunks were replaced by 3 ms time-bounded
> slices that run inside the frame's rAF callback, with a contention backoff. The sections below
> describe round 0 unless they say otherwise; the current design and its measurement are in
> "Fix round 1" at the end.

## Outcome

Solved shapes are now recorded, invisibly, in the three keys P-01 registered:
`arrows_shapes_seen_lo`, `arrows_shapes_seen_hi` and `arrows_shapes_through_level`. Recording ships
ON behind the literal kill constant `COLLECTION_SYNC_ENABLED = true` (`src/featureFlags.ts`). Nothing
on screen changes; the only future reader is the flagged gallery (W4-09).

- **Campaign clear.** After `setCurrentLevel(levelIndex + 1)`, `SaveSystem.recordCampaignClear(levelIndex)`
  runs the O(1) fast path: only when `through === levelIndex` does it OR in that level's bit and move
  `through` to `levelIndex + 1`. Otherwise it writes nothing, and the next menu visit folds the level.
- **Daily clear.** `registerDailyClear(day, shapeName)` also ORs in the cleared board's bit. It never
  moves `through`.
- **Menu.** `HomeScreen` starts the incremental fold `SaveSystem.syncCollection(maxSteps, shouldStop)`
  over `[through, currentLevel)`. The fold begins 2 s after the mount and folds at most 5000 levels per
  mount; leaving the menu cancels it.
  - Round 0 ran it in 200-level chunks, one per frame boundary.
  - Fix round 1 runs one slice per frame inside rAF. A slice stops after about 3 ms of work, and at
    most 200 levels.
  - After a late frame or a slice longer than 6 ms, the fold backs off 30 frames.
- **Never recorded.** Tutorial boards, benchmark (PERF) runs and losses set no bit.
- **`newlyDiscovered`.** Both clear methods return `{ newlyDiscovered }`, which is true only when this
  clear set a bit that was 0. GameScreen keeps the value in `newlyDiscoveredRef` for W5; this task
  shows nothing.
- **Version-aware lookup.** W3 has not landed (`src/core/generatorVersion.ts` does not exist), so the
  fold's lookup `campaignShapeName` in `saveSystem.ts` is v1 `shapeNameForLevel` for every index.
  That function is the single seam W3-05 reroutes through `resolveGenVersion(index, genSwitchLevel)`
  (ruling F11).

## Boot and frame-time measurement

All numbers are EXECUTED on the API 31 emulator (`fleet_floor_api31`, 4 vCPU, 60 Hz, 1440×3120) under
Hermes, using a release bundle.

**How the builds were made.**

- Measurement APKs are JS repacks of the working tree over the 87ec519 testads release APK
  (`scripts/repack.sh`; native 87ec519..HEAD is unchanged).
- The W4-06 handback flag set was used, minus `META_BANNER`: a test banner loading at a random moment
  in the window is a confounder, and it was off in both arms.
- The instrumentation was applied only for the build and then restored (`scripts/build-measure.sh`
  plus `scripts/instrument.py`; the tree sha was checked before and after, MATCH).
- Every bundle was proven before install: Google TestIds present, and the owner's
  `ca-app-pub-9813131856455133` and its four unit numbers absent, in both ASCII and UTF-16LE
  (`apk/*.proof.txt`).

**How the runs were made.**

- Seeding: the release APK is not debuggable, so `run-as` was unavailable. The seed used
  `adb -s emulator-5556 shell su 0 sqlite3`, confirmed on this image, on `RKStorage` (AsyncStorage
  2.2.0's SQLite database, table `catalystLocalStorage`). The app was force-stopped first, and
  ownership and SELinux labels were restored afterwards.
- Each run was a cold start seeded with `arrows_current_level = 5000` and the collection keys deleted
  (`scripts/measure-run.sh`). ON and OFF runs were interleaved.
- Clock alignment: `performance.now()` equals CLOCK_MONOTONIC ms, matching logcat `-v monotonic` to
  within 0.9 ms in every run. So HWUI `framestats` `IntendedVsync` can be windowed exactly from the
  JS menu-mount stamp.

**Instruments.**

- JS thread: `requestAnimationFrame` intervals for 4.5 s after the menu mount.
- UI thread: HWUI framestats rows with Flags = 0, from three dumps with no reset, merged and
  de-duplicated.
- Fold cost: `performance.now()` around every `syncCollection` call.
- GC: Hermes `getInstrumentedStats`.

Analysis: `scripts/analyze.py`, `scripts/summary.py`, and `artifacts/W4-07/measure2/summary.txt`.

### Cost of the fold itself

- **One-shot, 5000 levels in one call:** 128.4 and 129.9 ms on the JS thread (`measure/oneshot-*`),
  about 26 µs per level. That is far more than one frame, so the fold had to be chunked.
- **Hermes allocation:** about 6.6 KB per level (1.32 MB per 200-level chunk, 33 MB per 5000). A
  5000-level fold triggers 9-10 young-gen GCs totalling 2.0-3.3 ms, so GC is not where the time goes.
- **Final chunks (200 levels):** median 4.75-5.35 ms, max 6.3-10.0 ms, 0 of 125 chunks above
  16.7 ms. The 25 chunks total 121-140 ms per 5000 levels. The fold finishes about 0.85 s after it
  starts, one chunk every other frame, because RN's `setTimeout(0)` fires on the next frame.
- **Incremental on device** (`measure-incremental/inc-*`): seed 12000, then four launches.

  | launch | levels folded | `through` after |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 5000 | 5000 |
  | 2 | 5000 | 10000 |
  | 3 | 2000 | 12000 |
  | 4 | 0 (one O(1) call, 0.012 ms, 0 KB allocated) | 12000 |

  The level-0 seed also costs one 0.015 ms call and writes nothing.

### Why the fold starts 2 s after the mount (the replaced design)

The first implementation started the fold right after the first menu frame (`measure/`,
`measure-gc/`, 4 ON vs 4 OFF runs, interleaved). It regressed the menu's first 2 s:

- JS rAF max: ON 54.6–405 ms vs OFF 30.9–42.7 ms, with non-overlapping ranges in 4 of 4 runs.
- 9 of 100 chunks took over 16.7 ms (29–56 ms). GC was not the cause: where it was measured
  (`measure-gc/on-1`), the 29 ms chunk contained one 0.13 ms GC.

What was happening:

- The chunks overlapped the start-up burst: AdMob, WebView and test-ad preloads.
- On a 4-vCPU emulator, the JS thread was preempted mid-chunk. The 405 ms case was a whole-app stall
  (`EGL_emulation app_time_stats max=383.73ms`).
- In flag-off runs, long JS frames occur up to about 1.6 s after the mount.

Moving the start to 2000 ms (`COLLECTION_SYNC_START_DELAY_MS`, OWNER-PICKED STARTING VALUE) removed
the regression. The per-chunk max dropped to ≤ 10 ms.

## Wipe risk and migration

- **What this task writes.** Only the three collection keys, and only by OR-ing bits in and moving
  `through` forward. No other key is written, modified or deleted by W4-07 code.
  - There is no `deleteKey`, no `multiRemove`, no schema bump, and no change to `migrate()`,
    `resetProgress()` or `PersistenceKeys`.
  - Proof: EXECUTED jest assertions that every `setInt` in the collection paths targets only these
    three keys and that pre-existing keys are byte-identical on the mock disk.
  - On device, the non-collection keys were identical before and after every ON run (5/5) and every
    OFF run (9/9), except `arrows_tel_session_count`, which W6-05 increments on every cold start.
  - OFF runs wrote no collection key.
- **Before and after the first launch.** EXECUTED on device and in jest:
  - **Fresh install:** no collection key is ever written until the first campaign clear. That clear
    writes `lo` and `through = 1`.
  - **Level 37, no collection keys:** after the menu has been open for 2 s or more, `lo = 19051199`
    (14 shapes, equal to the full-generator truth) and `through = 37`. `hi` is not written (all 26
    catalogue ids are in `lo`). If Play is pressed within 2 s, nothing is written yet; the next menu
    visit folds it.
  - **Level 5000:** `lo = 67108863` (all 26; the truth has all 26 seen by level index 167) and
    `through = 5000`.
  - **Level 12000:** 5000 levels per menu visit, so it takes three visits.
  - **Corrupt or partial:**
    - `through = 'garbage'` does not parse at hydrate and reads 0, so it is refolded (refolding only
      re-ORs).
    - `lo = -5` reads as 0 and is rewritten with the truth.
    - A mask with bits of 30 or above keeps its low 30 bits, and is rewritten only when a bit is
      added.
  - **A key from a FUTURE catalogue:** `hi = 33554432` (index 55) survives untouched (device), and so
    does `lo` bit 29 (jest). Unknown bits are never cleared or shifted.
- **Failed hydrate** (`persistenceHealthy = false`): no collection write. Writing would OR into
  default zeros and overwrite the real collection on disk. EXECUTED `storage.test.ts`: the disk keeps
  `lo = 1023` and `through = 37`.
- **Downgrade to a pre-W4-07 build:** the keys are hydrated (registered since P-01) but never read or
  written, so they are inert.
- **Rollback:** `COLLECTION_SYNC_ENABLED = false` stops every collection write (EXECUTED jest
  kill-switch test, and the OFF device runs). The stored ints sit inert.
- **`resetProgress()`:** unchanged. It deletes the six legacy progress keys and keeps all three
  collection keys (EXECUTED `saveSystem.test.ts`). After a reset, `through > currentLevel`, so
  recording pauses until the player passes the old pointer. Under v1, replayed levels deal the same
  shapes, whose bits are already set, so nothing is lost.

## Decisions

1. **`registerDailyClear(day, shapeName)` takes the cleared board's shape** (`level.shapeName`). It
   does not regenerate `generateDaily(day)` at clear time, which is a full board generation, and it
   records exactly the board the player cleared.
   - This changes W4-06's test "writes only `arrows_daily_last_day`". It now asserts the day write
     plus exactly the Plus bit, and makes the clock throw, which strengthens "never reads the clock".
   - The other three W4-06 calls only gained the argument.
2. **All collection writes require `persistenceHealthy`.** This is P-01's rule for derived values;
   see "Wipe risk and migration".
3. **Corrupt values.** Masks read through `sanitizeMask`, and `through` through `nonNegativeInt`. A
   mask is written only when it gained a bit, so a corrupt value is repaired only by a real addition.
4. **An unmapped name sets no bit, but the fold still passes that level.** The build-time test
   forbids unmapped names for campaign 0..199 and dailies 0..364.
5. **`newlyDiscovered` is false when the clear is deferred.** A bit that the pending fold may set
   cannot honestly be called new.
6. **5000 levels is the per-mount budget.** With chunking there is no longer one "call", so the
   brief's `maxSteps = 5000 per call` became the budget for one menu mount. The chunk size (200) was
   measured.
7. **A new file, `src/ui/collectionSync.ts`, outside the brief's Files list.** It is needed for the
   chunking and deferral the controller required. The scheduler is pure (injected frames) and
   node-tested.
8. **`src/ui/contrastAudit.ts` is outside the Files list.** Its test pins HomeScreen and GameScreen
   line numbers, so adding lines broke it. The site references were re-pointed mechanically, as
   W4-06 did.
9. **No `src/core/index.ts` export.** W4-09 can import `src/core/collection`.
10. **Measurement builds had `META_BANNER` off in both arms.** The handback APK has it on.

## Concerns

- **JS p95 +3 ms during the fold window (round 0; resolved by fix round 1, where the medians are
  18.6 vs 18.85 ms).** The fold window (about 0.85 s, once per backfill, starting
  2 s into a menu visit) raises the JS-thread p95 frame interval by 3.0 ms at the median (21.8 vs
  18.8). This replicates, and the UI thread is unchanged. It is the one metric outside the null
  spread; controller or owner call.
- **The start delay can hold the backfill.** An upgrading player who always taps Play within about
  2 s never runs the backfill. While `through < levelIndex`, their new campaign clears are deferred
  as well.
  - Nothing is lost: the fold covers every level below `currentLevel`.
  - But a gallery (W4-09) opened before a 3-second menu visit would show an incomplete collection.
    W4-09 should run or await `syncCollection` when the gallery opens.
  - Fresh installs are unaffected, because the fast path keeps them in step from the first clear.
- **The Hermes lookup is heavy.** It costs about 26 µs and 6.6 KB of garbage per level. An
  allocation-free first-draw helper in `DotNetRandom` would cut it, but it touches the RNG core used
  by generation (golden checksums). Out of scope; possible follow-up.
- **Pre-existing, not W4-07: a failed hydrate can overwrite progress.** After a failed hydrate,
  `HydratedIntStore` still writes through, so a clear writes `arrows_current_level` over the real
  saved value. The new `storage.test.ts` case shows that `multiSet` call. Worth its own task.
- **W3-05 obligation.** Reroute `campaignShapeName` in `saveSystem.ts` through `resolveGenVersion`.
  A progress reset under a v1→v2 switch may deal different shapes on replayed levels; W3-05 should
  decide how the fold treats that.
- **UNVERIFIED-DEVICE.**
  - The fold's cost on a mid-range phone. Chunk 200 leaves about 3× headroom under a frame.
  - The 2 s start-up burst on real hardware.
  - iOS was not run.
- **Installed APK is a debug-signed repack.** An upload-key gradle build needs
  `adb -s emulator-5556 uninstall com.danteb.arrows` first, which wipes the test data.
- **No native build ran.** Free disk is 3.5 GB, below the 5 GB gate, so every build was a JS repack.

## Fix round 1 — time-bounded slices (controller review, 2026-09-25)

**Finding addressed.** In round 0, the JS frame-interval p95 during the 2–4 s fold window was +3.0 ms
(5/5 runs), which conflicts with the owner's "no fps gets worse" rule.

**Mechanism.** Each 200-level chunk (about 5 ms) ran from `setTimeout(0)`. On RN Android that fires
at the next frame, ahead of that frame's rAF callbacks, so it pushed them back by the chunk length on
alternate frames. Halving the chunk (100 levels, round 0's sensitivity arm) barely helped: p95 was
20.6–22.1 against 19.0–19.5.

### Change

- `foldLevels(..., maxSteps, shouldStop?)` and `SaveSystem.syncCollection(maxSteps, shouldStop?)`
  take an optional stop predicate. It is checked before every level after the first, so a slice
  always folds at least one level. Stopping early is just a smaller call, so the incremental
  `through` semantics are unchanged.
- `startCollectionSync` now runs one slice per frame inside the rAF callback. A slice stops at the
  first level boundary after `COLLECTION_SYNC_SLICE_MS = 3` ms on `performance.now()`
  (OWNER-PICKED), capped at 200 levels in case the clock does not move. This adapts to the device:
  a phone 3× slower folds a third as many levels per frame.
- **Contention backoff** (OWNER-PICKED): no slice on a frame that arrives more than 25 ms after the
  previous one, and after a slice that took more than 2× its budget, 30 frames (about 0.5 s)
  without slicing.
  - Why: the first design of this round (slices only, `measure3/`, 6 ON vs 6 OFF) left two runs with
    a JS max of 30.5 and 43.6 ms.
  - In both, a slice had been preempted: one level took 8.8 and 17.7 ms. In on-4 that coincided with
    the app creating an ad video decoder (`MediaCodec`, 1.94–2.0 s after mount), and in on-2 with
    netd activity.
  - The backoff keeps one such hit from turning into several stretched frames.
- Unchanged: the 2 s start delay, the 5000-level per-mount budget, the fast path, the daily bit, the
  kill constant, the health gate, and all persisted-data behaviour.
