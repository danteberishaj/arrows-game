## Method (EXECUTED)

1. `plan210.ts` (run on a `git archive HEAD src` copy, so another agent's uncommitted `src/core` edits cannot change the
   board): every blocked arrow of level index 0 and 3827 with its cells, direction and straight run, and every arrow's
   cells (for registration).
2. `cap210.mjs` per level: seed the campaign pointer, cold launch, Play, `[ftue] board_mount` proves the board; two
   ArrowsWorkload pinch-outs clamp at fit, then two pinch-ins clamp at max zoom. In each state: a full-res screencap, the
   board model registered on it (`reg210.py`: the cell size from `boardCamera.ts`' formulas, the origin measured from
   stroke occupancy + connections; agreement 0.88–0.99 vs runner-up 0.75–0.94), and 7 `input tap`s on one blocked
   arrow's head 1.2 s apart during a 720x1560 screenrecord. **Every tap was a blocked tap: 7/7 `[ftue] blocked` lines in
   each of the 4 conditions** (the first one charged, `true`; the rest free, `false`).
3. `ana210.py`: per trial, the displacement of the arrow's own cells against the rest frame (template shift on the ink
   weight map, sub-pixel), the bump curve evaluated at the trial's real frame times (the sampled-peak prediction), the
   frames inside the 300 ms window against 0.3 s x the recorder's measured rate, and the rest stroke width on the
   full-res screencap. The first (charged) tap turns the arrow into the rose missed mark, so the 6 free re-taps are the
   measurement.

## Decisions

1. **Measured on the shipped base APK** (`w506-on.apk` = HEAD JS with the test flag set) instead of a `da93dcd` build:
   the brief's baseline is "the shipped behaviour"; the lunge code is unchanged since then (the controller's "Since this
   brief was written" note); no build was needed.
2. **Max zoom targets were chosen after registering the zoomed camera** (index 0: head (10,14) pointing down; 3827: head
   (18,26) up) instead of the fit targets: the pinch-in did not keep its focal point under the two-pointer injection
   (in the first attempt the max-zoom taps hit a different blocked arrow: its logcat showed a second `blocked true`; those files were overwritten by the re-run).
3. **Primary metric = template shift of the arrow's own cells** (not the brief's ink centroid), because the centroid is
   contaminated on dense boards (Acceptance 2); both are reported.
4. **More trials than planned:** 7 per condition (the first 3-trial run had low recorder rates under load; it is kept,
   superseded, in `cap/first-3-trials/`).

## Concerns

1. Not a defect, a harness note: with `META_ZOOMED_CAMERA` on (this build), the fit camera centres the board in the
   area ABOVE the navigation bar (POLISH-T2 `cameraViewport(…, bottomInset)`; registered origin y 951 px = centring in
   305..2952), not in the full 3120-px layout that P-02's `cellCenterForBoard` assumes (1035.7 px). A harness that taps
   from that formula on a zoomed-camera build lands 1.25 cells low on level 0 at fit; with the flag off (the harness
   convention) the formula holds. The W2-10 taps came from the registration.
2. **UNVERIFIED-DEVICE:** legibility on a physical phone at arm's length; the recording is half resolution.
