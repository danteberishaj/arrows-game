## Concerns

1. **The Unity-era arrow-shape figures are false, and DESIGN.md now leaves them out rather than
   replacing them.**
   - A scratch count, not committed, run with `npx tsx` on a scratchpad file over level indices
     0..599 found 60,458 arrows: 57.3% bent (spanning more than one row and more than one column),
     12,172 of length 1 and 11,734 of length 2 (39% of arrows at length ≤ 2), and a maximum length
     of 20.
   - The old doc said "length ~4–6, hard-capped", "~60–70% bend" and "zero long straight stripes".
     The code's rules (`targetLen` in `[4, 6]`, mop-up while length < cap + 2) would suggest at most
     8. So some path makes longer arrows, for example level index 128 has a 10-cell arrow. I did not
     debug that; it is outside this task.
   - DESIGN.md now describes the generation rules with citations and says outcome shares are "not
     measured by a committed script". W3 or W5 should own a committed measurement and decide whether
     the length-1/2 share is a legibility problem.

   102–197, Super Hard 54–209) came from an uncommitted script over an unknown range. Over the
   brief's range 0..599 the script prints Normal 57–132, Hard 99–197 and Super Hard 47–224. DESIGN.md
   quotes the script, as acceptance 6 requires.
