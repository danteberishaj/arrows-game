## Outcome

With every W4 menu flag ON, from the first solve on:

- **One entry row** under the Play pill: `Today's board   ·   Gallery`. The two `inkDim` text controls are split by
  the stats line's own `   ·   ` separator. The separator is the whole gap between the two words: the facing side of
  each control has no padding, and the outer side keeps the 16 dp that widens its ≥ 44 × 44 dp hit box. The separator
  is plain text, not focusable and not read out. When only one flag is ON, its control shows alone, with no separator.
  Before the first solve the row is hidden, and the menu tree is BASE's.
- **The column is placed in the free room.** The column holds the wordmark, `Level N`, the tier, Play and the row. It
  is centred between the header buttons and the stats band. The stats band is two stats lines at the system font
  scale plus a fixed 56 dp banner reserve. The column is placed once per mount, so a banner that loads (or fails)
  never moves it; EXECUTED, see "banner arrival". W4-06's 36 dp stopgap is gone: the gap from the tier to Play is
  BASE's 56 dp again.
- **The stats line keeps its anchor**, 32 dp above the inset, lifted over the banner by the POLISH-T9 transform. It
  now wraps centred inside 18 dp side margins, and only after a separator. Before, the composite stats line ran
  edge to edge and flush left, and it split `best perfect run / 4`.

With every flag OFF, the menu is BASE's: jest trees are identical to BASE-written snapshots, and device pixels are
0 px changed outside the status bar, in 7 state pairs at both sizes.

## Decisions

1. **Column placement: centred in the free room**, not the screen. At 360×640 there is no screen-centred placement
   that passes the no-overlap criterion with the streak-saved line (BASE fails by 8.7 dp at font 1.0, and W4-06's
   stopgap already spent 20 dp of the tier→Play gap). Centring in [header bottom, stats band] passes at font 1.0 and
   1.3, and gives the tier→Play gap its BASE 56 dp back. The room is a fixed calculation per mount: two stats lines
   at the system font scale, plus a 56 dp banner reserve where the menu may show a banner. So a loading or failing ad
   never moves the column. That matters because "no motion other than the breathing" rules out a re-centre.
2. **`composed` = solved ≥ 1 and (META_DAILY or META_GALLERY or META_STREAK_FREEZE)**, read once per mount.
   - Before the first solve the menu is BASE's (the row and the stats line are both empty).
   - With only META_STREAK_FREEZE, the column is placed the composed way on every mount, not only on the one mount
     that shows `streak saved`. So the menu does not jump for one visit.
3. **The separator is the whole gap**: facing paddings are 0, outer paddings stay 16 dp, and the separator is the
   stats line's string at the entries' size (15 sp) with the entries' 4 dp top margin, so its dot centres on their
   labels. The brief asks for "the stats line's separator"; keeping W4-06/W4-09's 16 dp paddings on the facing sides as
   well would put about 32 dp + the separator (~27 dp at 15 sp) between the words (computed, not captured).
4. **Stats line on the composed menu**: 18 dp side margins (the header buttons' right inset) and `textAlign:
   center`. Breaks only after a separator: no-break spaces inside each part and before each dot. This is gated on
   `composed`, so the flags-OFF string is byte-identical. This is a layout change to W4-02's line, **not a copy
   change**: the characters shown are the same.
5. **Measured, not picked:** `STATS_LINE_PER_SP = 1.25`. Two lines measured 32.0 dp at 1.0 and 42.0 dp at 1.3, a
   ratio of 1.23–1.24. The banner reserve 56 is the measured 360 dp banner and is marked OWNER-PICKED; the 411 dp
   banner is 64. `STATS_BAND_LINES = 2` and `STATS_SIDE_MARGIN = 18` are OWNER-PICKED STARTING VALUES.
6. **`fontSize: 13` stays a literal in `styles.stats`**: `contrast.test.ts` reads that line. `STATS_FONT_SIZE` is a
   named copy, with a comment.
7. **Builds**: JS repacks only (`scripts/repack.sh`, host = the PERF-DEADTAG handback APK), `--reset-cache`, Metro
   cache deleted with `find` (0 left).
   - Every bundle was proven before install, in ASCII and UTF-16LE (`apk/*.proof.txt`): owner unit strings
     `ca-app-pub-9813131856455133`, `6706292920`, `7549521178`, `3505414519`, `5508761320` 0/0; Google
     `ca-app-pub-3940256099942544` present.
   - The inlining of flags and the menu literals is recorded in `apk/*.env.txt`.
   - At most 3 of my APKs on disk at any time; OFF builds were deleted right after their captures, and their sha256
     values are kept in `apk/sha256-all.txt`.

## A failure found on device and fixed (EXECUTED)

The first build with the column wrapper (iteration 2) passed every jest test and every `uiautomator` bounds check.
But **a tap on ♪ did nothing**: `arrows_sound_on` stayed 1 and the glyph was not struck.

- **Evidence:** `layout/header-tap-bug/`, where `crops.png` shows before, after the ♪ tap, and after a ☾ tap at
  x = 1000 outside the box, which worked.
- **Cause:** the column's padded box is drawn after the header and reaches over it (x 165–915 px, from y = 78), so
  Android's hit test picked the transparent column View and the press never reached the button.
- **Fix:** `pointerEvents="box-none"` on the column. Guarded by a jest test (mutant M3) and closed on device by
  `layout/controls-smoke/`.
- **Rule for later briefs:** a layout wrapper drawn after other controls must be `box-none`, and bounds checks do not
  catch this; only a tap does.
