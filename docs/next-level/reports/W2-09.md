## Decisions
- The worklet captures a module-local copy of the flag. The compiled closure confirms it.
- The capture APKs are built from an isolated `git archive` tree.
- Displacement is measured in the arrow's straight cells, away from the head, because both blockers (arrows 4 and 0) sit directly past the tapped arrows' heads and flash there.
- The colour floor is taken from reduced-motion heart frames: the same build and region, a known pure-heart state.
- For "settled", the arrow's own final colour is used as the reference (as T6 does), not the palette hex. H.264 shifts thin-stroke colours: the measured Daylight heart is (232, 75, 125) vs the palette `#E4327D`.
- `off-s1-a` was discarded and replaced by `off-s1-d`.
- I added the reduced-motion Ink Night sessions so Ink Night gets its own heart reference.
