## Harness defect found (fixed in the artifact, not re-run)

- **What happened.** `w321.mjs` first detected a foreign window from `mCurrentFocus` only. W7-09's lesson already
  said that a test interstitial keeps an app-package focus line. In B3 the ad was therefore treated as "the app".
- **Impact.** No tap was sent while the ad was up: the driver only polls UI dumps after "Next", and the run
  force-stopped.
- **Fix.** The driver now also reads `mResumedActivity` and rejects `AdActivity`. The fixed driver was syntax-checked
  but **not re-run on device**.
- I should have consulted the lessons file's W7-09 entry before writing the driver. The appended lesson says so.
