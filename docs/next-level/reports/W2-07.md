## Decisions

1. **Flag in `featureFlags.ts`** (controller note; `motionFlags.ts` does not exist).
2. **With the flag ON, the pip's pop effect is a `useLayoutEffect`.** The first ON build used `useEffect`, as the loss
   pop does. Its capture (`cap/probe-useEffect-build/on-refill-day-refill-manualback-pips.txt`) showed one frame of the
   refilled pip **filled at full size** before it jumped to 0.74 (+2.618 s: 29 px filled, +2.637 s: 22 px). Cause
   (INFERRED): the refill commit comes from a passive effect (the panel's exit settling), so its own passive effects
   are flushed after paint, one frame late; a tap's commit (the loss) has them flushed before paint, which is why the
   loss pop never showed this. With `useLayoutEffect` the first filled frame is already under-scale (acceptance 2). The
   loss pop in the ON build is unchanged (acceptance 3). OFF keeps `useEffect` (a build-time choice, so the hook order
   never changes) and is pixel-identical to BASE.
3. **The refilled pip stays spent while the panel fades** (`left = 0` while `refillPending`), so the fill and the pop
   land in one commit after the panel is gone. OFF recolours at `earned`, as before.
4. **`refillPending` rather than bumping the token inside the presence latch:** GameScreen reads the latch's published
   state (`useSyncExternalStore`), so the bump follows the `hidden` render; W2-05's latch code is untouched.
5. **BASE comparisons use the OFF build** (pixel-identical to HEAD + W5-03) instead of a `da93dcd` build.

## Concerns

1. **Decisions 2 goes beyond the brief's letter** (it names only `useEffect`'s rule, not the hook). It fixes a
   measured one-frame artefact and is confined to the flag-ON build.
2. **Perf resolution, and one late refill.** See the perf section. In one of five ON perf runs (A2, not counted) the
   refill landed more than 1.6 s after BACK: the panel was gone and the refilled pip still spent in the screenshot about
   3 s after BACK. The cause is not established. In the three capture runs (`cap/on-refill-day*`,
   `cap/repeat/on-refill-day-r2*`, `-r3*`) the pop started 18–44 ms after the panel's last frame. If W2-05's exit latch
   ever settles late, the ON build shows the refilled heart as spent until it does (OFF would show nothing, since it
   fills at `earned`). A phone check of the continue flow is the resolving test.
3. **The refill captures depend on Google's rewarded test ad**, a two-ad pod here (≈ 75–150 s); the harness waits a
   fixed 150 s before BACK because uiautomator cannot read the ad's "Reward granted". Live AdMob fill and the real
   reward timing are UNVERIFIED-DEVICE.
4. **Observed, not caused by this task:** while the rewarded ad is up, the game's `[board-viewport]` changes from
   `h=804.29` to `h=828.29` and back after BACK (`perf/invalid/A2-logcat.txt`, and every refill logcat): the game
   screen re-lays out under the full-screen ad and again on return, and each `onLayout` re-fits BoardView's camera to
   the opening camera. So a player who panned or zoomed loses that camera after any rewarded ad (INFERRED from
   `fitToViewport` being called from `onLayout`; not captured with a panned board). Under reduced motion one recorded
   frame right after the ad closes has the whole window about 20 dp higher (`cap/on-refill-rm-refill.mp4`, +2.069 s),
   the same inset hand-off seen on screen. Both are pre-existing ad-flow behaviour, independent of this flag.
5. **UNVERIFIED-DEVICE:** feel on a physical phone; iOS.
