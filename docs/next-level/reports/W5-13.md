## 1. The vector

**How it was built.** The four face colours were sampled from `mark.png` as the per-face median of
2 px-eroded flat regions (spread p5–p95 ≤ 5 per channel):

- light violet `#6C49E5`;
- dark violet `#5130C2`;
- magenta `#E63C7F`;
- dark magenta `#BA2469`.

The raster has no gradients: inside each face the standard deviation is about 2 per channel.

1. Nine connected regions were crack-traced and reduced with Douglas-Peucker to 3–7 vertices each.
2. Those were merged into 26 shared, named vertices. Each face is drawn as a polygon over those vertices.
3. Topology was fixed with collinearity constraints. For example, the dark band's corner and the dark-magenta
   sliver sit on the left bar's inner edge.
4. Coordinate descent refined all vertices together. It minimised the premultiplied RGBA difference against
   `mark.png` at 8x supersampling.

The scripts and the intermediate geometry are in `artifacts/W5-13/geometry/`. The SVG has no curves and no
traced blob: 9 faces, 20 polygons in total once the 11 underlaps are counted.

**Seams.** Faces that abut along an edge leak background into the antialiased pixels on that edge. To stop it,
each back face carries an "underlap" subpath in the same `<path>`. Its winding matches, so it is filled under the
nonzero rule. The underlap is a strip up to 5 units wide along the shared segment, clipped to the front face and
kept 0.75 units clear of that face's other edges.

Measurement (`artifacts/W5-13/geometry/seams.ts`): pixels differing by more than 16 from an 8x-supersampled render.

| render size | 24 | 56 | 108 | 192 | 512 | 1024 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| plain abutting faces | 22 | 61 | 112 | 189 | 578 | 1,103 |
| with underlaps (shipped) | 17 | 13 | 18 | 23 | 31 | 57 |

The pixels that remain are at points where three faces and a hole meet (the apex, the hole corners).

## 2. Fidelity gate (EXECUTED: `npx tsx scripts/art/mark-audit.ts`)

`mark.svg` rendered at 512 by sharp/librsvg, compared with `26096d3:assets/images/mark.png`. Values are
premultiplied RGBA, 0–255.

| measure | value |
|---|---|
| mean abs difference per channel, whole frame | 0.312 (R 0.248, G 0.210, B 0.351, A 0.441) |
| mean abs difference per channel, emblem px only | 2.277 |
| max abs difference per channel | 255 (A), 180 (B): edge pixels, see the rim below |
| emblem px (alpha > 0 in either) / 1 px edge band / emblem px outside the band | 35,944 / 10,054 / 28,031 |
| **after a 1 px (3x3 box) blur, px differing > 24 outside the edge band** | **0 = 0.000 %** (target < 2 %) |
| after a 1 px blur, px differing > 24 inside the edge band | 1,264 = 12.6 % of band px |

The edge band is every pixel within 1 px (8-neighbourhood) of a label change in the ref: transparent, or one of
the four face colours.

All large differences sit on edges. The biggest come from a near-black rim on the raster's up-left-facing edges,
which the vector does not redraw (RULING 1). The rest are corner rounding at the apex, the side bevels and the
bottom tips. The vector keeps the side bevels as 5-unit chamfers; its tips are sharp.

Size side-by-sides (old resized with lanczos3, the new SVG rendered at that size, both on white and on Ink Night,
plus |diff| x4) are in `artifacts/W5-13/sheets/size-{24,56,108,192,512}.png`. The map of pixels that fail the gate
outside the band is `fidelity-outside-band.png`; it is empty.

**Detail the paths could not match** (numbers above):
- the 1 px dark rim (RULING 1);
- sub-pixel rounding of the three tips and the side bevels;
- the raster's interior alpha of 254 (the vector is 255, a difference of 1).

Nothing inside the faces needed more paths.

## RULINGs

1. **The near-black rim is not redrawn.**
   - Evidence (`artifacts/W5-13/geometry/fringe.py`): edge pixels on the up-left-facing edges (150°–270°) have
     median luminance 1.4–1.9 against 91–92 for the face, at alpha about 60. The second ring in is about 50
     against 92. Edges facing other ways are within 0.69–1.04 of the face (0.54 in the 120–150° transition).
   - Where it shows: a 1–1.5 px dark line on the left bar's outer edge and on the hole edges that face left.
   - Reading: it looks like matting or colour/alpha misregistration from the generated raster, not shading. It is
     one-sided, near-black and about 1 px at any scale.
   - Effect of dropping it: the silhouette is about 1 px slimmer on those edges (`mark.png` bbox x0 0.230 → 0.234).
   - If the owner wants it back, it is one extra dark path along those edges.
2. **The face colours are the raster's own medians,** not the palette's `accent`/`heart`, per the 2026-10-06
   ruling to keep the emblem as it is. The `#6040e0` / `#e04080` hex pair in W5-11 is a 32-wide histogram bucket
   label, not a colour.
3. **The splash image stays 512 px.** The controller brief says current sizes. The original W5-13 text in W5.md
   said 1024; that was not followed.
4. **The monochrome layer is regenerated too.** It is an emblem raster with the same 1.72x upscale (W5.md lists
   it). The brief named the launcher, adaptive foreground, splash and favicon only.
5. **`scripts/generate-store-assets.js` no longer writes the emblem rasters** (see "What changed"). It was not
   run in the tree, where it would create an untracked `store/fallback/`. It was run once against a scratch copy of the tree to
   check that it works; see the gates.
6. **`assets/splash-icon.png` is untouched.** It is a 1024 px palette PNG from the initial commit, and nothing
   references it (`git grep splash-icon` is empty). That makes it dead weight, and it is not mark-derived output.
7. **W5.md's gate "the owner's written yes on the side-by-side, quoted in the commit message" is not met here.**
   The launcher icon is store-facing. The commits are on a review branch; merging them is the controller's call
   after the owner sees `owner-sheet.png`.
