touch `fitToViewport` or the camera (`git diff` of `BoardView.tsx` and `boardCamera.ts` is empty).

  - `captures/{off,on}/short-L17-{daylight,inknight}.png` and `short-L92-*.png`;

- What happens there today: the arrow under the button cannot be tapped at that spot. It **can** still be removed
  through its visible part. The tap-through test removed exactly such an arrow, and the ink under the button dropped

- Choices for the ruling, none built: accept short phones as they are; hide the button only when the fit board's
