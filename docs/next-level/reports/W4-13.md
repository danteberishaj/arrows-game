Card format (the owner's mock-up, the real card for 2026-10-06 as the share sheet received it):

```
Arrows · 2026-10-06
Pentagon

⬜⬜⬜⬜🟪🟪⬜⬜⬜⬜
… (9 rows of 10)
```

**RULINGs**
1. **Ceiling 280 UTF-16 units**: the rulings doc's suggestion (measured 261 with a 3-digit streak, plus room for 4
   digits). Measured here over days 0..3649: 261 (streak 365), **262** (streak 9999); max 10 rows, max 10 columns.
2. **Control style:** "a plain text Share control" in the spec; built as a secondary outlined button under Done (Retry's
   audited look) so it reads as a control without competing with Done. OWNER-PICKED STARTING VALUE: the label "Share".
3. **No "?" exemption needed:** no URL was ruled in, so the test forbids `?`, `&`, `utm_`, `http`, `www.`,
   `play.google` and `.com` outright, and asserts the only numbers are the date, hearts and streak (so no level index).
4. **Streak** = `SaveSystem.dayStreak` read at the tap, after the daily clear was registered (the number the player
   sees in the menu).
