2. Panel edges at the HC target use the audit's own alternative rule (fill OR hairline), as `audit()` does at AA.

4. Pressed states (accentDeep) keep today's pressed/rest contrast step, not the bare solver minimum (OWNER-PICKED).

## Concerns

1. **Android's own "High contrast text" also re-colours text.** With the setting on, Android draws all text in
   black/white with an outline (`hc-on-light.png`: "Level 2" is outlined black, not `accentLight`). So on Android the
   text tokens in our HC palettes are mostly overridden by the OS; our change is visible on fills, borders, hearts and
   icons. INFERRED from the captures; owner look check.
2. Arrow ink does not change in HC (already ≥15:1). If the owner expects the arrows themselves to look different,
   that is a new ask.
3. In Daylight on this host APK the 3-button nav bar is almost invisible (white on white, `hc-*-light.png` bottom):
   D3's native scrim is off (NAV-BAR plugin) while `META_NAV_BAR_THEME` was not in the brief's flag list. Not TF-02's
   change; a capture artefact of mixing the D3 host with NAV_BAR off.
4. Back and Hint stay reachable to the screen reader behind the won/lost panel (`a11y-won.xml`), unchanged behaviour.
5. Emulator: a "System UI isn't responding" dialog appeared once at first launch (host load); I tapped Wait
   (`device/start-menu.png`).
6. A uiautomator dump returned "null root node" once right after a cold launch; the retry 5 s later succeeded.

## Lessons for the controller

- The brief's device a11y check ("no clickable node lacks content-desc or text") cannot fail on RN Android for a
  Pressable with a Text child: Android merges the child text into the node's description, so the HEAD build passes it
  too. Check the `class` (Button vs ViewGroup) or use the jest sweep (role + label + state) as the gate.
- Android "High contrast text" overrides app text colours itself; an HC palette's text rows matter mainly on iOS.
- `wm density` restarts this activity (density is not in `configChanges`): play the level at the target size instead
  of resizing a shown panel.
- A board centre calibrated on one square level (CY 1712 at 1440x3120) transferred to another square level of a
  different size; at 1080x1920 it was 1096, derived from the ink bbox ratio of the same level at both sizes.
