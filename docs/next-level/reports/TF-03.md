### Screens: what needed a change

Every screen in the brief was captured on G3 and G4 with the flag ON. Only one layout rule needed a change:

- **Board camera, landscape only.** With `META_ZOOMED_CAMERA` on (every vc18 build), a board whose fit cell is under
  23.5 dp opens zoomed to about 14 cells across the viewport WIDTH. In landscape that asks for 1280 / 14 = 91 dp cells,
  capped by `maxScale` at 64 dp. EXECUTED pre-fix (`artifacts/TF-03/camera/prefix-g3-level6.png`, level 6, G3): a
  128 px (64 dp) row pitch, so about 10 of the board's rows show. The fix counts the 14 cells across the short side
  in landscape. EXECUTED post-fix (`camera/postfix-g3-level6.png`): 87 px (43.5 dp) pitch, against 611 / 14 = 43.6 dp
  modelled from the camera viewport (1390 px layout minus the 84 dp bottom inset). Portrait viewports, phone or
  tablet, are unchanged (jest pins `on` equal to `off` for every portrait viewport x board in the suite, and for an
  800x1100 dp portrait).
- **Unchanged and working as they are** (EXECUTED by capture + checks below): splash, menu (composed and first-run),
  banner area, board at fit, won panel with Share, lost panel, Settings sheet, skin picker, collection book, gallery,
  tutorial board. They already read `useWindowDimensions`/flex layout, and the board re-fits on any viewport change
  (`cameraOnViewportChange`).

## Decisions

- **Camera in landscape** (behind the flag): "14 cells across" counts across the short side. Without it, landscape
  opened large boards at the 64 dp cap. Taste-adjacent, so it is listed for the owner (see Concerns). Large PORTRAIT
  was left as is (800 / 14 = 57 dp), because changing it needs a new number.
- **Tutorial capture:** the arms do not enable `META_SETTINGS_HOW_TO_PLAY`, so "How to play" does not exist in them.
  T1 was reached as a first-run player instead (save made fresh, then restored).
- **The lock is evaluated once, in `onCreate`**, as the brief specifies. The activity handles size changes itself
  (`configChanges` includes `smallestScreenSize`), so it is not recreated when the window changes. See Concerns
  (foldables).
- **Acceptance exemptions** (Back/Hint hitSlop, scroll-clipped rows, sheet backdrops, the ad creative) are written into
  `scripts/accept.py` and printed per capture. The hitSlop one is proven on device, not only read from code.
- **Re-signed copies** for `install -r` over the debug-signed install, as NAV-BAR did; bundle hashes match.
- Fixtures were copied from the `android/` tree the last stamped build left (flags off), i.e. the real generated
  `MainActivity.kt`.

## Concerns

1. **Zoomed opening on large portrait screens is very zoomed** (unchanged by this task; owner call). On G4, level 6 (Star,
   155 arrows) opens at 800 / 14 = 57 dp cells, about twice the phone's 29 dp, showing a small part of the board
   (`G5/g4-board.png`; `ink.py` FAILS it by design). The same rule in landscape gave 64 dp before my fix, 43.6 dp
   after. A rule such as "never zoom past the phone's cell size" would need an owner-picked number.
2. **Foldables on Android 12-15**: the phone lock is set in `onCreate` only. A phone-sized fold that is unfolded
   without recreating the activity keeps portrait until the next launch (the system would not rotate it). Android 16
   ignores the lock at sw ≥ 600 anyway. A re-evaluation in `onConfigurationChanged` would fix it; it is outside the
   brief. UNVERIFIED-DEVICE.
3. **Gallery on a wide screen** shows 16 small columns (`G3/gallery.png`): it works and nothing is clipped, but the
   tiles stay phone-sized. Looks like a candidate for the owner's eye, not a defect.
4. **Banner height after a failed first load (pre-existing, not landscape-specific, INFERRED):** once, the banner
   painted without reporting a height, so the stats line stayed under it (`G3/menu.png`, `G3/menu.xml`). It happened
   right after the system_server restart; a relaunch was correct (`G3/menu-banner.png`, `G4/menu.png`). Possibly
   `MenuBanner` sets 0 on `onAdFailedToLoad` and a later automatic refresh does not fire `onAdLoaded` with a size. Not
   reproduced on demand.
5. **UNVERIFIED-DEVICE:** a real Android 16 tablet/foldable (where the system ignores the lock), Chromebook free-form
   resizing, split-screen, and iOS (no change made). Only the API 31 emulator with `wm size`/`wm density` was used.
6. The emulator was unstable under host load: two system_server deaths (00:19, 01:28) and SystemUI ANRs. None was
   attributable to our app (the first was a system `FinalizerWatchdogDaemon` timeout; our process died with
   `DeadSystemException` like every other). Evidence taken while it was unhealthy was discarded and retaken.

## Lessons for the controller

- `dumpsys window | grep mGlobalConfiguration` can return a stale configuration after `wm size`; `am get-config` and
  `dumpsys window displays` (`cur=… app=…`, `mRotation`) gave the live values.
- A G2 rotation test needs a positive control: the launcher itself is portrait-locked, so "the display stayed
  portrait" proves nothing until an unlocked activity (Android Settings) rotates under the same settings.
- uiautomator reports drawn bounds, not `hitSlop`; a 36 dp `HeaderButton` is a 52 dp target. Prove a slop by tapping
  outside the drawn bounds.
- uiautomator dumps fail ("could not get idle state") on the menu while the Play pulse runs and a banner WebView is
  live; retrying up to 4 times got a dump in most cases.
- On a loaded host, host-side screencaps (~1 s each) miss the splash; back-to-back on-device `screencap` calls in one
  `adb shell` catch it. `screenrecord` stopped after < 0.5 s here, on the device and over `exec-out`.
- Tapping "the middle of the board" to lose hearts can land on the lost panel's "Continue (ad)" button once the panel
  appears; tap one blocked arrow at a time and check the dump between taps.
- The phone lock must be checked with the APK's dex, not only the prebuild output (`dexdump`: the `smallestScreenWidthDp`
  read, `#int 600` and `setRequestedOrientation`), as the fingerprint gate decides whether a prebuild ran at all.
