### Method: bracket each span with the neighbouring frames (offline, same recordings)
- The spans never used the tap time. D is frame-to-frame inside the recording, so the host-sent tap being a loose anchor does not affect D.
- The open question was whether screenrecord delivers the onset frame late or drops it. `artifacts/W0-07/scripts/w004-bracket.mjs` (gitignored) re-reads the unmodified `analyse.mjs` per-frame tables. Heart counts are bimodal, split at 300 as in `w004-bimodal.mjs`. For each span it gives:
  - onset ∈ (t_prev, t_first], where t_prev is the last recorded frame **without** heart in that region;
