## Decisions

1. **One entry per ROLE, not a merged scale.** Roles with equal values stay separate (menuTier / menuEntry /
   galleryCount are all 15 semi 0.5): merging them into a scale is the hierarchy question the brief leaves to W5-20.
2. **Only the keys that existed** (no `letterSpacing: 0`, no family for the ★ glyph), so a spread adds nothing.
   `gameTutorial` carries bold and spacing 1: the tutorial line's style array already inherits them from
   `levelLabel`, so the flattened style is identical (checked in the trees below).
3. **The Skip / Claim / Close labels of the dev-only ad modal have no fontSize** (RN's default); they stay without one.
   W5-02's icon sizes (`CONTINUE_HEART_DP`, `STREAK_SPARKLE_DP`) are icon sizes, not text sizes, and stay W5-02's.
4. **sizeSite points at the use site** (the spread line), and the test resolves the token, rather than pointing at
   theme.ts: the audit keeps proving that the style it names renders at that size.
