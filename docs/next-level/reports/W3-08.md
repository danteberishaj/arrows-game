## Decisions

1. **The board comes from the seeded campaign pointer (`arrows_current_level`), not `EXPO_PUBLIC_DEV_LEVEL`.**
   - **Why:** DEV_LEVEL is inlined at build time, so seven boards would need seven APKs, against the three-APK cap and
     4 GB free.
   - **What W3-06 still supplies:** its `DEV_GEN_VERSION` switch picks v1 or v2, one APK each.
   - **Proof:** `board_mount` shows every board.
2. **The build flags are minimal, with every META flag off.** This is the shipping look, and it means no ZOOMED_CAMERA
   and no BOARD_GRID, per the dispatch note. Every capture's `manifest.json` records the flag set.
3. **The fit camera came from a capture-only log line, not from a pinch-out.** The line lives in the scratch tree only
   (`artifacts/W3-08/scripts/board-camera-diag.patch`) because nothing tracked logs the camera scale. It was not
   committed, since tracked edits are limited to the doc and the hitTest comment.
4. **A companion taps Play.** `capture.mjs` has no step that opens a level on a non-PERF build. The companion waits for
   the `screen=menu` log and taps Play once at coordinates calibrated by uiautomator. `capture.mjs` itself is unmodified.
5. **Two boards were added beyond the brief's four.**
   - **v2's extremes over levels 1–10000**, as the dispatch note asked: index 245 (Triangle 43×43), the largest board,
     and index 1787 (Fish 37×44), the smallest cell.
   - **One mid-size v1 board, index 4 (Diamond 29×29).** It gives the owner a step between 16.9 pt and 9.1 pt.
6. **The first 46×46 board is index 389, found with the probe's rows mode.** `--shape-report` has no per-level size
   output; its clamp-hit column is consistent.
7. **The owner sheet is ordered by logged cell size** (at 360×780), which here is also board size. It shows both themes
   and both geometries as thumbnails, plus two 1:1 device-pixel crops, because a thumbnail understates density.

## Concerns

1. **The brief's premise is wrong in two places.**
   - Index 11 (Bolt 46×37) is the **tallest** v1 board, not the one with the smallest cells. Cells are column-bound.
   - Level 390 (Crescent 46×46) is the smallest, at 7.36 pt. By level 6 (Star 44×44) cells are already 7.69 pt on a
     360 dp phone, and 107 of v1 levels 1–1000 are below Bolt's 9.15 pt.
   - Its "514×1114 dp" is the wrong-density figure W1-01 already corrected.
2. **The screenshot set is not sent to the owner.** I did not message anyone; sending is the controller's outward-facing
   step. The sheet is `artifacts/W3-08/owner-legibility-boards.png` (2444×5396, 1.7 MB) and carries the exact question.
3. **An instrument trap in `capture.mjs`, noted rather than fixed.**
   - **What happens:** its `finally` resets `wm` after the screenshot. The app re-lays itself out at the intermediate
     `1440 px @480` configuration and logs a second `[board-viewport] w=480 h=949` line after the shot.
   - **Why it matters:** anyone reading "the last logged viewport" of a wm-override capture gets the wrong number. My
     first parse did exactly that; the PNG was fine.
   - **What I did:** my companion takes the log snapshot before the shot and records `cameraLinesBeforeShot`, which was
     1 in all 29 captures.
   - **Candidate for P-02 tooling:** record the screencap timestamp in the manifest, or read the log before the reset.
4. **Observation for W3-09 / W3-10, not a judgment.** v2's median cell is smaller than v1's: 12.53 pt against 14.10 pt
   at 360 dp over levels 1–1000. v2 has fewer boards below 8 pt (8 against 25) but many more between 8 and 14 pt.
5. **All captures are from one software-GPU emulator.** Legibility on a real 6-inch phone at arm's length is still
   UNVERIFIED-DEVICE (W3-22).
