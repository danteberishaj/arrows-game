   published, ADMOB-C). A draft note (dropped from the page) says it must be resolved (P-1)
   before publication. Resolving it is the owner's decision (data-safety.md §10.1), not a wording
   fix.
2. **The US opt-out by email cannot be applied to ad requests** without the gate / UMP US
   message; the legal review decides whether it is adequate (data-safety.md §10.2).
3. Every vendor statement rests on Google's disclosure page as read on 2026-09-26.
