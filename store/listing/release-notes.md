One section per release, newest first. Paste the block under "en-US" into Play Console's "Release notes" field
(limit 500 characters per language). Each line names only what is new since the previous release; check it against that

## 1.0.0 (18) — 2026-10-13, tag release/vc18 (45c08f6)

New since vc17: META_THEME_SYSTEM, META_HIGH_CONTRAST, META_SETTINGS_HOW_TO_PLAY, META_SHARE_CARD_LEVEL,
META_SETTINGS_RATE, META_NAV_BAR_THEME, plus the TF-02 accessibility label sweep. 406 characters.

en-US:

```
What's new:
• Match phone theme: Arrows can follow your phone's light or dark setting.
• High contrast: stronger colours when your phone's high-contrast setting is on.
• How to play: replay the tutorial any time from Settings.
• Share any cleared level, not just the daily board.
• Rate Arrows from Settings.
• The navigation bar now matches the game's theme.
• Better screen reader labels on every button.
```
